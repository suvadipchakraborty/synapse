Synapse

The living constellation of human knowledge.

Synapse turns any academic field into an explorable graph — built on open metadata from OpenAlex, rendered with a physics simulation, and designed to make discovery feel like drifting through a night sky of ideas.

Install Synapse

Add it to your home screen like a native app.

  1. Tap the Share icon ⬆ in Safari's toolbar.
  2. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add — Synapse now opens full-screen from your home screen.
